    👑 Finance Minister

    The minister gets a panel on their own balance page with classmates' names (never balances) to make deposits and withdrawals you ask for. Each one comes to you as a request to approve or reject. Choose the minister with the 👑 button on the Students tab.

    Currency logo

    Add a picture for your class currency (a coin, a mascot, your class logo…). It shows on each student's balance page and next to balances here. Square images with a plain or transparent background work best. It's shrunk automatically.
