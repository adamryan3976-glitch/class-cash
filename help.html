Class Cash: Teacher Guide

Getting started

Class Cash is a paperless classroom economy: you add and take away class money from a laptop or phone, and each student checks their own balance through a private link. Every deposit or withdrawal can also be tagged with an Ontario Learning Skill, so the history doubles as evidence for report card learning skills.

What you need:

  • Your @ddsb.ca Google account. Personal Gmail accounts can't sign in.
  • The Class Cash address: class-cash.winchesterps.ca. Bookmark it on your laptop and add it to your phone's home screen.
  • Your class list.

What students need: nothing to install and no sign-in. Each student gets their own link, which shows only their balance and history.

Step 1: Sign in and create your class

  1. Go to class-cash.winchesterps.ca and tap Sign in with Google.
  2. Choose your @ddsb.ca account. If you pick another account, you'll see a message asking you to use your board account.
  3. The first time, you'll see Welcome! You're not in any classes yet. Tap Create a class.
  4. Type a class name, such as Room 12 or Grade 5 French, and tap OK.
  5. Class Cash opens the Students tab so you can add your class right away (Step 3).

If you're a rotary teacher who only works in other teachers' classes, you don't need to create a class. Ask each homeroom teacher to add you (Step 5), and their classes will appear when you sign in.

Teach more than one class? Use the class menu in the green bar at the top. Pick + New class… to make another, and switch between classes from the same menu.

Step 2: Set up your class

Open the Settings tab, make your choices, and tap Save settings. Only the class owner (the teacher who created the class) can change these.

Setting What it does Example

Currency name What your class money is called Maple Bucks
Symbol Shown before amounts, up to 4 characters $

Show learning skills on students' balance pages Students see the skill tags beside each entry in their history On

Currency logo (optional). In the Currency logo box, tap Choose image… and pick a picture, such as a coin, a mascot or your class logo. It's shrunk automatically and appears on each student's balance page and next to every balance on the Bank tab. Square images with a plain or transparent background look best. Tap Remove to take it away.

Logos aren't private: anyone with a student link can see them. Never use photos of students.

Step 3: Add your students

  1. Open the Students tab.
  2. In Add students, type one student per line: the name you want them to see, then (optionally) their student email.
  3. Tap Add students. They appear under Your class right away.
Jordan L.   S123456789@ddsbstudent.ca
Sam P.      S987654321@ddsbstudent.ca
MS

Tips:

  • Use any name you like: first names, first name and last initial, or initials. That's exactly what students see on their page, so it's a good way to keep full names out of the app.
  • The email is optional and only teachers see it. It helps you match records; students never type it.
  • From a spreadsheet: copy two columns (name, then email) and paste them into the box. Both are read correctly.
  • New student mid-year? Add them the same way any time.
  • If a name is already in the class, Class Cash asks before adding it again.

Step 4: Give students their private links

Each student has their own link with a random code that can't be guessed. Opening it shows only that student's name, balance and history, and it updates live. No sign-in is needed.

To get links, open the Students tab:

  • Copy link copies one student's link.
  • Preview opens the page exactly as the student sees it.
  • Copy all links copies a Name / Email / Link table. Paste it into a private spreadsheet that only you can see.

Ways to share a link:

  • Google Classroom: paste it into a private comment to that student, never in the class stream.
  • Class Chromebook or iPad: open the link and bookmark it, or add it to the home screen.
  • Younger students: print each student's link on a card and keep it somewhere they can reach.

Tell students: “This link is just for you. Don't share it.” If a link does get shared, reset it (see Managing students). The old link stops working immediately.

Step 5: Add rotary teachers and other co-teachers (optional)

Rotary teachers, prep coverage or a teaching partner can deposit and withdraw in your class without seeing anyone else's classes.

  1. Go to Settings → Teachers.
  2. Type the teacher's @ddsb.ca email, exactly as they sign in, and tap Add teacher.
  3. Tap Copy invite beside their name and paste the message to them. It includes the Class Cash link.
  4. When they sign in, your class appears in their class menu, marked (co-teacher).

Everyday use: deposits and withdrawals

Everything happens on the Bank tab, and it works the same on a phone or a laptop.

  1. Pick students. Tap one student, tap several, or tap Select all for the whole class. Selected students turn green with a ✓. Use Find a student… to search, and Clear to start over.
  2. Choose an amount. Tap a quick-amount button or type any amount, including cents.
  3. Tag learning skills (optional). Tap one or more of the six coloured buttons: Responsibility, Organization, Independent Work, Collaboration, Initiative, Self-Regulation. On a laptop, hover over a button to see its sample behaviours.
  4. Add a comment (optional), for example Helped clean up after art. Comments you've used before are suggested as you type.
  5. Tap ＋ Deposit or − Withdraw.

A message at the top confirms what happened, for example Added $5 for Jordan L. (Responsibility, Collaboration). The students, amount, skills and comment then clear, ready for the next one. The student's page updates within seconds.

Good habits for report cards:

  • Tag the skill the behaviour shows, both for deposits (strengths) and withdrawals (next steps). A withdrawal tagged Self-Regulation is evidence just like a deposit.
  • Add a short, specific comment to anything you may want to quote later. Stayed on task during independent reading is more useful in January than no comment.
  • Whole-class rewards can be tagged too. Each student gets their own entry.

Finance Minister (optional)

The Finance Minister is a student job: one student makes deposits and withdrawals when you ask, and you approve each one. They never see anyone's balance, and the job can move to a new student each month in one tap.

Turn it on (class owner):

  1. Go to Settings → Finance Minister and tick Let one student be the Finance Minister.
  2. Set Most a minister can enter at once (for example 5). Bigger requests are refused.
  3. Optional: tick Approve minister requests automatically while a teacher has Class Cash open.
  4. Tap Save settings.
  5. On the Students tab, tap 👑 Make minister beside the student. Their row shows 👑 Finance Minister.

What the minister does: a Finance Minister panel appears on their own balance page, under their balance. They tap one or more names (or Select all), type the amount and a reason, then tap ＋ Deposit or − Withdraw. They can include themselves. Each entry is sent to you as a request, and balances don't change until you approve it.

Approving requests: they appear at the top of the Bank tab under 👑 Finance Minister requests.

  • Tap learning skills on a request first if you want to tag them, or leave them blank. Only teachers can tag learning skills.
  • Tap Approve or Reject on each one, or Approve all.
  • A withdrawal is held, shown in red, if the student doesn't have enough money (unless negative balances are allowed).
  • Approved entries show 👑 and the minister's name in History, and Finance Minister on the student's page.

Changing ministers: tap 👑 Make minister beside the new student. The previous minister's panel disappears straight away, and requests they already sent stay in your list. To end the position, tap 👑 Minister ✓ on the current minister, or untick the setting.

With automatic approval on, requests go through within a second or two whenever any teacher of the class has Class Cash open. Withdrawals that would overdraw a student still wait for you. You can undo anything from the History tab.

Fixing mistakes: History and Undo

The History tab lists the 100 most recent transactions, newest first. Each one shows the date and time, student, amount, comment, learning skill tags, whether it was a group or whole-class entry, and which teacher made it (you for your own). Use the menu at the top to show one student only.

To fix a mistake, tap Undo on that line and confirm. The transaction is deleted and the balance is corrected, on the student's page too. Then redo it correctly on the Bank tab.

A whole-class deposit creates one line per student, so undoing it means one Undo per student. For anything older than the latest 100 transactions, use the Skills tab.

Report card time: the Skills tab

The Skills tab collects every deposit and withdrawal tagged with a learning skill. Use it to choose each student's E / G / S / N marks and to find specific examples for comments.

Set the dates. From starts at September 1 of this school year and To is today. For the fall progress report or a single term, change them to that reporting period.

Whole class overview (the default) shows a table with one row per student and one column per skill: ▲ counts deposits and ▼ counts withdrawals tagged with that skill. Use it to spot students with very few entries in a skill. Tap a student's name to open their report.

One student's report (choose them in the Student menu) shows:

  • A summary table: for each skill, how many deposits and withdrawals, their totals, and the net.
  • A section for each skill with every tagged entry: date, amount, comment, and the teacher who made it. An entry tagged with two skills appears under both.
  • At the bottom, a collapsed list of transactions with no learning skill.

Print gives a clean page with no buttons or menus. Choose Save as PDF in the print window to keep a copy for your records or for a parent conversation.

The six skills and their sample behaviours, as on the Ontario report card, are under Learning skills and sample behaviours at the bottom of the tab.

The report is evidence to support your professional judgement, not a mark calculator. A student with more deposits isn't automatically “Excellent”.

Managing students

On the Students tab, tap More beside a student:

Option What it does Who can
Edit name / email Changes the name the student sees, or their email. Their link stays the same Owner and co-teachers
Reset link Makes a new link. The old one stops working immediately; balance and history are kept. Share the new link with the student Owner and co-teachers
Delete student Removes the student, their balance, history and link for good. Can't be undone Owner only

Reset a link when a student shares it, loses it, or someone else has been looking at it. When a student moves away, print their Skills report first if you'll need it, then delete them.

Moving over from the old Google Sheet (optional)

If you used the Class Cash Google Form and Sheet template, you can bring over every past transaction so balances carry on where they left off.

  1. Add your whole class first on the Students tab. Whole-class entries from the sheet go to every student in your Class Cash list, so anyone missing would miss them.
  2. In your old sheet, open the Form Responses 1 tab and choose File → Download → Comma-separated values (.csv).
  3. In Class Cash, go to Settings → Import from the old Google Sheet and choose the downloaded file.
  4. Check the matching table. Each name from the sheet (for example Lee, Jordan) is matched to a Class Cash student (Jordan L.) where possible. Fix any wrong matches, pick + Add as a new student if needed, or leave a name on skip.
  5. Tap Import transactions and confirm.
  6. Compare a few balances with the old Class Bank Balance tab.

Import only once: importing the same file twice doubles every amount. Imported entries have no learning skill tags.

End of year and privacy

At the end of the year:

  1. Print or save as PDF any Skills reports you want to keep.
  2. As the owner, go to Settings → Delete this class → Delete class… and type the class name to confirm. This permanently removes every student, balance, link, transaction and the logo, for all teachers in the class.
  3. In September, create a new class.

Keeping student information private:

  • Students only ever see their own balance. There's no page that lists other students.
  • Only teachers of a class can see its class list, emails and history.
  • Use first names or initials if you'd rather not store full names.
  • Share links privately, never in a class-wide stream or on a shared screen.
  • Don't use student photos as the currency logo.
  • Sign out of Class Cash on shared computers (Settings → Sign out).

Troubleshooting and FAQ

Problem or question What to do
“Please sign in with your @ddsb.ca account” You picked a personal Google account. Sign in again and choose your board account
A co-teacher can't see my class Check that the email under Settings → Teachers matches the account they sign in with exactly, then have them refresh the page
“Not enough …” when withdrawing The student's balance is lower than the amount. Use a smaller amount, or turn on Allow balances to go below zero (owner only)
“You don't have permission to do that” That action is owner-only (settings, logo, teachers, deleting). Ask the class owner
A student's link says “this link isn't working” The link was reset or the student was deleted. Copy their current link from the Students tab
A student's link just says “Loading…” The device or network may be blocking the site. Try another device; if it still happens, contact the Class Cash organizer
Something looks out of date Refresh the page. Class Cash also reconnects on its own after Wi-Fi drops
Can I use it on my phone? Yes. Add the site to your home screen for one-tap access
Can students change their balance? No. Their link is view-only
Does a deposit need a learning skill? No. Skills and comments are both optional
I teach Kindergarten Turn off Tag deposits and withdrawals with learning skills in Settings
The Finance Minister says “That didn't go through” They may no longer be the minister, the position may be turned off, or the amount is over your limit. Check Settings and the Students tab

Still stuck? Contact the staff member who looks after Class Cash at your school.
